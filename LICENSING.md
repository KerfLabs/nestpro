# Using nestpro: who is free, who pays

nestpro is licensed under the
[PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/small-business/1.0.0).
This page explains it in plain words. If this page and `LICENSE` ever disagree,
`LICENSE` is what counts.

## Free: small businesses

Your business uses nestpro free, for real production work, if **both** of these
are true:

- **Fewer than 100 people** work for it, counting employees **and** independent
  contractors.
- **Less than US$1,000,000 total revenue** in the last tax year. That is the 2019
  figure; it rises with US inflation, so it is somewhat higher today.

Free use covers everything: installing it on as many PCs as you like, nesting and
cutting for paying customers, changing the code, and sharing it (with `LICENSE`
included).

**Count the whole group.** If a parent company, sister company or owner controls
your business, add their people and revenue to yours. A 20-person shop owned by a
500-person group is a 520-person company.

## Paid: larger companies

Above either line, you need a **commercial license from Kurf Labs**. Write to
**hello@kurflabs.com** with:

- the company name, and how many people and sites will use it;
- which machines you program (laser, plasma, oxyfuel, waterjet, and the brand);
- anything you need that it doesn't do yet.

Pricing is kept small and simple. Companies that contribute (code, machine
post-processors, test files) may get a license free or at a discount.

**Trying it first.** A larger company that wants to evaluate nestpro before
buying can ask for a free evaluation license at the same address.

## Common questions

**We were small and now we're bigger.** Keep working. Once your last tax year
puts you over a line, contact us for a commercial license.

**I'm using it at home, or to learn.** Write to us and we'll give you a free
license for that. The PolyForm terms only describe use by a business, so we
handle this case directly.

**We work for a big company as a subcontractor.** What counts is *your* company's
size, not your customer's. A 15-person job shop cutting parts for a large OEM
uses it free.

**Can we build on nestpro and pass it on?** A small business can change it and
share it, but everyone who receives it gets the same PolyForm terms. So a larger
company that receives it still needs a commercial license from us. If you plan
to build a product on nestpro, write to hello@kurflabs.com first.

**What about older copies?** Versions published before 8 November 2026 were under
the MIT license and stay MIT for anyone who already has them. Everything after
that date is under PolyForm Small Business.

**Is this open source?** Not in the strict sense, because larger companies pay.
The full source is public, and small shops can use, change and share it freely.
We call that "source-available".
